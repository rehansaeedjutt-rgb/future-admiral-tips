## 🚀 Overview

**Future Admiral — Daily Trading Tips** is an internal automation project developed exclusively for the **Future Admiral Trading Community**.

This system delivers a carefully curated trading tip every single day to our Discord server — covering technical analysis, fundamental analysis, risk management, trading psychology, and market wisdom.

This is not a public tool. It is a **private system owned and operated by the Future Admiral team**, designed to continuously educate and elevate our community members.

---

## 🎯 Purpose

In trading, **knowledge is the ultimate edge**. Most traders fail not because of bad strategies, but because of:

- Lack of foundational understanding
- Poor risk management
- Emotional decision-making
- Ignoring market fundamentals

To solve this problem, this system was built:

> **A daily drip-feed of trading wisdom that reaches every member of our community — automatically, consistently, and professionally.**

---

## ⚙️ What It Does

This system performs the following tasks automatically every day:

| # | Task |
|---|------|
| 1️⃣ | Selects a unique, high-quality trading tip |
| 2️⃣ | Categorizes it by topic and difficulty level |
| 3️⃣ | Formats it into a professional, visually appealing embed |
| 4️⃣ | Delivers it to the Future Admiral Discord community |
